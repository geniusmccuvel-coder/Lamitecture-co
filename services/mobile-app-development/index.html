When an app makes sense

Closer to your customers

An app sits on their phone, so coming back to you takes one tap.

Faster for repeat tasks

Booking, ordering and checking details become quick, which keeps customers returning.

Useful for your team too

Staff apps can handle jobs, schedules and updates from a phone instead of paperwork and calls.

What you get

We start with the problem, not the technology.

  • An app designed around your customers and your business
  • Customer accounts, bookings, orders or requests, depending on what you need
  • A matching website or admin area so you can manage what happens in the app
  • Plain advice on whether you need an app, a website, or both
  • Help with publishing on the app stores
  • Support and updates after launch

How it works

  1. We define the problem

    We work out what the app must do and who will use it.

  2. We design the screens

    We sketch and design how the app looks and flows, and you approve it.

  3. We build and test

    We build the app and test it on real phones.

  4. We publish and improve

    We help you launch, then improve the app based on how people use it.

Common questions

Do I need an app or a website?

Many businesses do very well with a website alone. An app makes sense when customers use you often, such as booking or ordering, or when your team needs a tool in their hands. We will tell you honestly which one you need.

Can one app work on both iPhone and Android?

In many cases yes. We recommend the best approach for your situation before we start.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on what the app does. We give you a clear quote after we understand your idea, and we can start with a smaller first version.

Will you publish it on the app stores?

We guide you through it. Store accounts and review rules take some care, and we help you through each step.
