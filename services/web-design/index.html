Common questions

How much does a website design cost?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the website has to do. After a short conversation we give you a clear quote, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the website and how quickly we receive your logo, photos and text. We agree a timeline with you before we start.

Do I need to provide the text and photos?

You can, and we will guide you on what works best. If you are missing something, tell us and we will work out a solution together.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Most customers will visit on a phone, so every website we design is built to look and work properly on small screens.

What is the difference between web design and web development?

Design is how your website looks and how it is organised. Development is the building work that makes it fast, secure and functional. We do both, so you deal with one team.
