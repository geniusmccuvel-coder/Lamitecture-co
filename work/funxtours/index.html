FUNXTOURS: a booking website for attractions, hotels and B&Bs

FUNXTOURS is a travel and tourism booking platform. People find attractions and places to stay across South Africa and book them in one place, and owners list and manage their own offerings.

The goal

Planning a day out or a weekend away usually means searching many different places. The goal was to make that simple: search once, compare attractions, hotels and B&Bs, and book.

Visit the live website

What we built

The features that matter most to the people using the website.

  • A homepage built around one question, Where to, with a search for any city, attraction or place to stay
  • Separate sections for Attractions, Hotels and B&Bs
  • Build My Trip, a helper for visitors who are not sure where to start
  • A Become a host area, so owners can list and manage their own offerings
  • Sign in for guests and hosts
  • A light and dark display option
  • A layout that works on phones, tablets and computers

The design

Gold and cream give the brand a warm, premium feel. A large serif headline and a simple search bar keep attention on the one thing visitors came to do.

Why it works

One clear action on the first screen: search.

Help for undecided visitors, through Build My Trip, so they do not leave.

A clear way in for both sides of the business: guests and hosts.
